---
name: Mobile conversion hierarchy
description: A responsive landing-page rule for keeping the first action visible on small screens.
---

On mobile-first sales pages, place the headline, supporting value proposition, and primary conversion CTA before a large product image in the reading order. Keep the visual prominent immediately after the CTA.

**Why:** A large hero image can consume the first mobile viewport and hide the action that matters most, especially when traffic arrives from messaging and social apps.

**How to apply:** Keep the product visual first or side-by-side on larger screens when it supports the composition; switch to copy-first ordering at the narrow breakpoint and verify the CTA appears without scrolling.
